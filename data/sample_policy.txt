================================================================================

SECTION 1: POLICY OVERVIEW

================================================================================

This health insurance policy provides financial coverage for medical expenses incurred due to illness, injury, or hospitalization, subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits described in this document.

================================================================================

SECTION 2: COVERED MEDICAL EXPENSES

================================================================================

2.1 Hospitalization Coverage

The insurer will cover expenses related to inpatient hospitalization, including:

Room rent and boarding charges

Doctor and surgeon fees

Nursing expenses

Operation theatre charges

Medicines and medical consumables

2.2 Pre- and Post-Hospitalization

Pre-hospitalization expenses: Up to 30 days before admission

Post-hospitalization expenses: Up to 60 days after discharge

2.3 Day Care Procedures

Medical procedures that do not require 24-hour hospitalization are covered, including:

Cataract surgery

Dialysis

Chemotherapy

Radiotherapy

2.4 Ambulance Charges

Emergency ambulance expenses are covered up to $1,000 per hospitalization.

================================================================================

SECTION 3: SUM INSURED AND LIMITS

================================================================================

Total sum insured per policy year: $200,000

ICU charges: Covered up to 2% of sum insured per day

Room rent limit: Single private room

================================================================================

SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS

================================================================================

4.1 Pre-Existing Diseases

Any illness or condition that existed prior to policy inception is not covered for the first 24 months.

4.2 Waiting Period

General waiting period: 30 days from policy start date

Specific diseases (hernia, gallstones, joint replacement): 12 months

4.3 Non-Covered Treatments

The policy does not cover:

Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures

Dental treatments unless due to accident

Experimental or unproven treatments

Infertility or assisted reproduction procedures

4.4 Self-Inflicted Injuries

Expenses arising from intentional self-harm or substance abuse are excluded.

================================================================================

SECTION 5: CO-PAYMENT AND DEDUCTIBLES

================================================================================

Standard deductible: $500 per claim

Co-payment: 10% of claim amount for insured persons above 60 years

================================================================================

SECTION 6: CLAIMS PROCESS

================================================================================

6.1 Cashless Claims

Available at network hospitals

Pre-authorization required before admission

6.2 Reimbursement Claims

To file a reimbursement claim:

Submit claim form within 15 days of discharge

Provide original bills, discharge summary, and prescriptions

Claims will be settled within 21 working days

================================================================================

SECTION 7: POLICY TERMS AND RENEWAL

================================================================================

7.1 Policy Renewal

Renewable annually on timely payment of premium

Lifetime renewability available

7.2 Policy Cancellation

Policyholder may cancel with 15 days’ written notice

Insurer may cancel due to fraud or misrepresentation

7.3 Portability

Policyholder may transfer this policy to another insurer as per regulatory guidelines.

================================================================================

SECTION 8: DEFINITIONS

================================================================================

Hospital: A registered medical institution with inpatient facilities

Pre-existing Disease: Any condition diagnosed before policy start date

Co-payment: Percentage of claim amount paid by the insured
